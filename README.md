## 📌 Executive Summary
This project is an end-to-end, cloud-deployed commercial intelligence platform designed to simulate a live executive reporting environment for Michael Hill International (MHJ). Moving beyond standard descriptive analytics, this hub integrates **Machine Learning (ML)** and **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** to provide predictive forecasting, autonomous customer segmentation, and real-time operational telemetry. 

The UI/UX is custom-engineered using CSS glassmorphism and an Onyx/Gold color palette to reflect a premium luxury retail brand identity.

---

## 🚀 Core Capabilities

### 1. 🤖 Brilliance AI (NLP Assistant)
A custom-built conversational data agent. Executives can query complex transactional datasets using natural English (e.g., *"What is the revenue for March 2026?"* or *"Which boutique has the highest sales?"*), bypassing the need for complex SQL queries.

### 2. 🔮 Predictive Demand Forecasting
Integrated `scikit-learn` Linear Regression models historical daily revenue and projects a 30-day forward-looking trendline, enabling proactive inventory and staffing allocation.

### 3. 👥 Unsupervised Customer Segmentation
Utilizes K-Means clustering AI to autonomously segment 50,000+ transaction rows into core buyer personas (e.g., *Lume LAB Bridal* vs. *Everyday Silver*), empowering targeted marketing spend.

### 4. 🌱 2025 ESG & Sustainability Telemetry
Maps commercial growth against corporate responsibility targets, specifically tracking the revenue shift towards the circular **Re:cycle gold program** and low-carbon **Laboratory-Grown diamonds**.

### 5. 🚚 Operational Latency & CX Tracking
Visualizes fulfillment bottlenecks across the physical boutique footprint and correlates delivery friction directly with regional Customer Sentiment (CX) scores.
